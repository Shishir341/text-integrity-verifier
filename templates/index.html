Understanding SHA-256 Hashing

What is SHA-256?

SHA-256 stands for secure Hash Algorithm with a 256-bit output. It converts text into a fixed-length hash value containing 64 hexadecimal characters.

How Does Text Intergrity Verification work?

The application generates a SHA-256 hash for each text entered by the user. It then compares the two hash values. If they match, the texts are identical. If they do not match, the text content is different.

What is the Avalanche Effect?

The avalanche effect means that even a small change in the original text can produce a very different hash value. For example, changing one letter or adding a full stop usually changes many characters in the SHA-256 hash.

Why is Hashing Important?

Cyrptographic hashinng helps detect changes in digital information. It is commonly used in cybersecurity and data integrity checking. However, comparing hashes alone does not prove who created the original text.
